# LF5.6: SDLC Frameworks & Methodologies

## Epic User Story

As IT Consultants, **we** want to evaluate and compare different Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) frameworks, so that **we** can choose the right management methodology for future projects based on their complexity, team size, and customer involvement.

## Celebration Criteria (Definition of Done)

- **We can evaluate** the strengths and weaknesses of classic vs. agile software development models. (K6)
- **We know how to map** the practical phases we completed (Requirements, Design, Coding, Testing) to the theoretical V-Model. (K5)
- **We can design** an analog Kanban/Scrum board to organize a development team's tasks visually. (K6)
- **We can compare** specific agile frameworks like eXtreme Programming (XP), Kanban, and Scrum. (K5)

## Comprehensive Task: The Framework Retrospective (10 SP)

**The Mission:** You have successfully analyzed, designed, built, and tested a prototype for your chosen scenario (Neustadt Museum, Mutti's Bakery, or Metropolis Library). Now, you must reflect on _how_ this process could be managed in the real world. You will compare how your journey would look under a strict classic model versus an Agile approach, and build an analog tool for your next project.

**Tasks:**

1. **The Classic Reflection:** Document in Docmost how your team's workflow from Epic 5.1 to Epic 5.5 maps to the phases of the classic **Waterfall Model**. Identify one major risk your specific scenario would face if the stakeholder heavily changed their mind during the Implementation phase (Epic 5.4).
2. **The V-Model Mapping:** Draw an analog **V-Model** on a whiteboard. Map your specific Epics to the model (e.g., place Epic 5.1 "Lastenheft" on the top left, and Epic 5.5 "UAT" to the corresponding verification phase on the top right).
3. **The Agile Transformation:** Compare how **Scrum** handles changing requirements differently than the Waterfall model for your scenario. Document this comparison in Docmost, focusing on the concepts of "Sprints" and "Sprint Reviews" with your stakeholder.
4. **Analog Board Design:** Use a physical wall/whiteboard and sticky notes to build a **Kanban Board** (Columns: Backlog, To Do, In Progress, Review, Done). Write at least 5 specific tasks from your previous Epics on sticky notes and place them in the correct columns as if the project was currently running.
5. **Framework Presentation:** Finalize your Docmost page summarizing the pros and cons of Waterfall, V-Model, Kanban, and Scrum for your specific scenario. Embed photographic proof of your analog V-Model and Kanban board.

**Result Artifact:** A "Methodology Retrospective" document in Docmost, featuring written comparisons of the frameworks, risk analyses, and embedded photos of the analog V-Model and Kanban board.
